Recovery Spool

With this project, I used Onshape to build models that I printed using a Bambu P1S 3D Printer, gaining experience with working in a workshop environment and improving my knowledge of prototyping and manufacturing methods and processes

Problem:

Attach a recovery line to a buoy anchor and return the line to the surface

Iteriative Process:

Redesigned every piece in phases

Solution:

Completed task in short amount of time: Achieving a Time Bonus

Fun Fact: EER is one of only three teams to get full points in the wave tank out of 86 total teams in the 2026 MATE ROV competition
